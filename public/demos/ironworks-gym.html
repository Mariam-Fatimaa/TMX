The space

8,000 sq ft of
proper kit

A converted engine shed on Kirkstall Road. High ceilings, roller doors open in summer, and equipment chosen by people who lift.

The rack floor

14 competition racks, 6 deadlift platforms, calibrated plates from 0.5 to 25 kg, and specialty bars including a safety squat bar and trap bar.

Turf lane

20 m of turf for sleds, carries and sprints.

Engine room

10 rowers, 8 air bikes, 4 ski ergs.

Changing & showers

Six private showers, lockers, hair dryers, towels on request.

Recovery corner

Foam rollers, bands and a cold plunge.
