Chef's story

A Lahore childhood, a Manchester kitchen.

My nani never measured anything. She tasted, added salt, tasted again. That's still the whole recipe.

Zara Malik grew up above her family's sweet shop in Anarkali, then spent twelve years cooking in London and Copenhagen before opening Saffron & Salt in a former textile warehouse in 2021. The food keeps the heat and generosity of home, with the restraint she learned in tasting-menu kitchens.

Masalas are toasted and ground in-house every morning. Vegetables come from two farms in the Cheshire plain, and the grill runs on British charcoal.

— Zara Malik, chef-owner
