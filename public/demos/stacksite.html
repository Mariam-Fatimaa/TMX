FAQ

Good questions, straight answers.

Still unsure? Our support team is real people, seven days a week.

Do I need any design or coding skills?

No. If you can write an email and drag a photo, you can build a Stacksite. Every block is designed to look good with your content, on any screen.

Can I move my existing website over?

Yes. Paste your current URL and we import your pages, text and images into a draft. Business plans include a free 30-minute migration call.

Can I use my own domain name?

Any paid plan lets you connect a domain you already own, or buy a new one inside Stacksite. SSL is set up for you automatically.

What happens if I cancel?

Your site stays online until the end of your billing period. You can export pages, CMS collections and form submissions as CSV at any time.

Is the free plan really free?

Yes — one site on a stacksite.app address, up to 5 pages, no time limit and no card required.
