How we work

No ticking clock. A price, a plan and a named lawyer.

We quote a fixed fee for most work. Where a matter genuinely can't be fixed, such as a contested trial, we give a stage-by-stage estimate and a cap you can hold us to.

  1. Free 30-minute call

    Tell us what's happening. We'll say whether we can help and roughly what it will involve.

  2. Written fee letter

    Within two working days: the scope, the fee, the timeline and the lawyer who will do the work.

  3. Regular updates

    A short update every Friday while your matter is live, and a direct line to your lawyer.

  4. No surprise invoices

    If the scope changes, we tell you first and agree any extra cost before we do the work.

FAQ

Questions people ask before they call.

Can't see yours? Call 0113 496 0218 and you'll speak to a person, not a menu.

Is the first consultation really free?

Yes. The first 30 minutes, by phone or video, costs nothing and commits you to nothing. If you'd like a longer in-person meeting with a partner, that is £150 plus VAT and is deducted from your fee if you instruct us.

What happens if my matter turns out to be more complicated?

We stop and tell you before doing any extra work. You'll get a revised quote in writing, and you can decide whether to go ahead. We never add hours to a bill without your agreement.

Do you offer legal aid?

No, we don't hold a legal aid contract. If you may qualify, we'll point you to a firm that does, usually on the same call.

How quickly can you start?

Most matters start within two working days of you signing the fee letter. For urgent work such as injunctions or tribunal deadlines, we can start the same day.

Can you act if I'm outside Yorkshire?

Yes. About a third of our clients are elsewhere in England and Wales. Meetings run by video, documents are signed electronically, and we travel for completions and hearings where needed.

How do you check for conflicts of interest?

Before we discuss details, we check the names of everyone involved against our records. That's why the booking form asks for the other party's name. It is kept confidential and used only for this check.
