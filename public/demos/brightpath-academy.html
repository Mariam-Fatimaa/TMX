How it works

A clear plan from the first week

No guesswork for parents. You’ll always know what your child is working on, and why.

  1. 1

    Assessment

    A 40-minute diagnostic in the trial class shows us exactly which topics are solid and which need work.

    Week 1 · free
  2. 2

    Learning plan

    You get a one-page plan: target grade, topic order, and how many sessions a week make sense.

    Within 3 days
  3. 3

    Classes

    Small groups or one-to-one, with past-paper practice built into every session and homework marked in 48 hours.

    2–3 per week
  4. 4

    Progress reports

    Every four weeks: a short written report, a mock score, and a 15-minute call with the teacher if you want one.

    Every 4 weeks
