      Screens

      Four screens your group will open most.

      We watched twelve groups plan real trips before drawing anything. These are the moments they kept coming back to: at the gate, on the day, at the dinner bill and when someone asks "where was that place?"

      Features

      Built for the trip with four opinions and one card machine.

      Every feature came from something that went wrong on a real group trip: a lost booking email, a forgotten adapter, a dinner nobody remembered paying for.

      Shared day-by-day plan

      Add a stop and the whole group sees it with travel time from the last one. Vote on maybes instead of arguing in the chat.

      Forward it, it's filed

      Forward booking emails to your trip address. Flights, stays and tickets land in Wallet with codes, times and the right day.

      Split as you spend

      Log a cost in two taps, split it evenly or by item, in any of 14 currencies. Tripnest works out the fewest payments to settle.

      Packing that reads the forecast

      Lists start from your destination's weather and plans. Shared items are assigned once so nobody brings three hairdryers.

      One map of everyone's saves

      Pins from the whole group on one map, filtered by day. Long-press to add a place to the itinerary.

      Works with no signal

      Passes, maps, the plan and the budget are stored on the phone. Changes sync when anyone's back online.

      Design notes

      Bright enough for holiday, ordered enough for a 06:25 boarding time.

      A warm paper background, one loud coral for actions, and a colour per kind of plan so a busy day can be read at a glance.
