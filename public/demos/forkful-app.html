      Screens

      Four screens that do the heavy lifting.

      Most orders touch the same four moments: choosing, customising, paying and waiting. We spent the most design time here.

      Key features

      Built for the people who cook, carry and eat.

      • Three-mile radius

        Only kitchens close enough to arrive hot. No dark kitchens, no virtual brands hiding behind one address.

      • 12% flat commission

        Restaurants see the fee on every order in their dashboard. Couriers earn per mile, plus 100% of tips.

      • Live courier map

        Position refreshes every five seconds once your order leaves the pass, with a timed ETA you can trust.

      • Dietary filters that stick

        Set halal, vegan or nut-free once. Menus hide what you can't eat and flag anything uncertain.

      • Talk to the kitchen

        Leave a note per dish. Kitchens can reply if they need to swap an ingredient, before they start cooking.

      • Reorder in two taps

        Your usual Friday order sits on Discover, with today's price and wait time already worked out.

      User flow

      From craving to doorstep.

      The happy path takes about 70 seconds and five screens. Each step was tested with 14 regular delivery customers in Edinburgh.

      1. 01

        Open & browse

        Discover opens on kitchens near your saved address, sorted by how soon food can arrive.

      2. 02

        Pick a dish

        Filter by cuisine or search a craving. Dietary settings already hide what doesn't fit.

      3. 03

        Make it yours

        Choose broth, extras and notes. The price updates before you tap add.

      4. 04

        Pay once

        Apple Pay, Google Pay or a saved card. Fees and tip are shown line by line.

      5. 05

        Watch it arrive

        Kitchen status, then the courier on the map, then a nudge when they're at your door.
