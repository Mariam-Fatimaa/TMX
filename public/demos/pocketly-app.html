    Screens

    Four screens people open every week

    Beyond the main tabs, these are the flows our testers came back to most: signing up, checking a payment, saving towards something and keeping a budget honest.

    Key features

    Built for the ten seconds people actually spend in a banking app

    01

    One balance, every account

    Everyday, savings pots and joint accounts add up on the home screen, with a weekly trend line underneath.

    02

    Automatic categories

    Payments are sorted into groceries, bills, transport and more as they clear. Fix one and similar payments follow.

    03

    Send in three taps

    Pick a person, type the amount, confirm with Face ID. Most transfers land in under ten seconds.

    04

    Card controls that stick

    Freeze a lost card, cap monthly spending or switch off cash withdrawals, and every change applies instantly.

    05

    Pots with a deadline

    Savings goals with a target date, weekly auto-save and round-ups from everyday spending.

    06

    Shared spaces

    Couples and housemates split bills from a joint pot and see who paid what, without the group chat maths.

    Design notes

    A dark interface that stays calm about money

    The app runs on a near-black canvas so balances and colours carry the meaning. Each spending category keeps the same colour everywhere it appears: chart, list, budget and notification.

    User flow

    Send money in 3 taps

    The old flow took seven screens. We cut it to three by remembering recent payees and moving confirmation into Face ID.

    1. 1

      Tap a face

      Recent and favourite people sit at the top of Pay. No sort codes for someone you've paid before.

    2. 2

      Type the amount

      A big keypad, available balance underneath and a gentle shake if you go over it.

    3. 3

      Confirm with Face ID

      One glance sends it. Sam gets a notification and you get a receipt in Recent.

    4. ✓

      Arrived

      Average time from first tap to money landing: 6 seconds across 40,000 test transfers.

    Try it on the phone above: open Pay, pick a contact and type an amount.
