Screens

Four screens that do most of the work

Most people open Carely to do one of four things: check a doctor, book a slot, join a call or find a prescription. Each of those gets a screen built around a single main action.

Key features

Built for the moments people actually book care

We spoke to 14 patients and 5 clinic receptionists before drawing a screen. Most bookings happen in the evening, on a phone, for someone else.

Two-tap booking

Pick a day, pick a time, confirm. Saved payment and patient details mean there is no form to fill in on the way.

Video consultations

A waiting room opens 10 minutes early with a camera and connection check, so the first minute isn't spent on "can you hear me?".

Medication reminders

Every prescription can switch on its own reminder. Times follow the dose the doctor wrote, and you can move them.

Family profiles

Book for a child or a parent from one account. Each person keeps their own records, and switching takes one tap.

Records in one place

Prescriptions, test results and consultation notes, sorted by date and searchable. Share a file with a new doctor in a tap.

Clear prices upfront

Every doctor card shows the fee and the appointment length before you tap in. No surprises at checkout.

Design notes

Calm, clinical, and still human

A soft periwinkle blue does the work of a clinic's white walls without feeling cold. Warm accents mark the things that need you: reminders, ratings and results.

User flow

Book a GP in under a minute

We timed this flow with 14 test users on their own phones. The median was 48 seconds from opening the app to a confirmed booking.

  1. Open Carely

    Home shows your next appointment and a "Book GP" shortcut.

    0:00
  2. Tap Book GP

    Doctors opens pre-filtered to GPs, sorted by soonest slot.

    0:06
  3. Choose a doctor

    Rating, fee and next free time are on the card, no need to open a profile.

    0:18
  4. Pick day and time

    Booked slots are struck through. Confirm unlocks once both are set.

    0:34
  5. Confirm

    Saved card, calendar invite and a reminder 30 minutes before.

    0:48
