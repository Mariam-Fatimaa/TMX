Getting here

Above the cove, below the village

The hotel sits on its own on the coast road between Port Esca and Vilaroca. We run a free transfer from Port Esca station for every train between 9:00 and 21:00.

  • Cala Morta cove, on foot25 min
  • Port Esca harbour & station12 min drive
  • Vilaroca village & market8 min drive
  • Santa Aurea airport55 min drive
