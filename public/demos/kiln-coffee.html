Farm to cup

We know who grew it, and what they were paid.

Most of our green coffee comes from the same nine farms every year. We agree prices before harvest, publish them, and visit each farm at least every second year. This is how a bag gets from their drying beds to your kitchen.

  1. 01 · Farm

    Picked ripe, by hand

    At Finca La Esperanza, Doña Marleny's pickers go over each tree up to five times so only red cherries get picked.

    Huila, Colombia · Jun–Aug
  2. 02 · Price

    £5.90 a kilo, agreed in March

    We pay for green coffee before it is picked. Last year that averaged 2.4× the Fairtrade minimum across all nine farms.

    Price list published every April
  3. 03 · Roast

    12 kilos at a time

    Our Probat drum roaster was built in 1964. Each coffee has its own profile, and we cup every batch before it is bagged.

    Kirkstall, Leeds · Tue & Fri
  4. 04 · Your cup

    At your door in two days

    Bags go out by Royal Mail the afternoon they are roasted. Rest them for five days, then drink within six weeks.

    Best from day 5 to day 42

How we brew it at the roastery.

Start with these recipes, then adjust: if it tastes sour, grind finer; if it tastes bitter or dry, grind coarser.

  1. 0:00Rinse the paper filter with hot water and tip the water away.
  2. 0:00Add 15g coffee, level the bed, and pour 45g water to bloom. Swirl gently.
  3. 0:45Pour slowly in circles to 150g, keeping the water level steady.
  4. 1:30Pour to 250g. Give the dripper one gentle swirl to flatten the bed.
  5. 3:00Drawdown should finish around three minutes. Longer? Grind coarser next time.
